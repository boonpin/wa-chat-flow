# Troubleshooting

## Cannot Login

- Verify credentials: `admin@admin.com / admin123`
- Make sure app is running on `http://localhost:3000`

## WhatsApp Not Connecting

- Retry **Connect WhatsApp**
- Refresh and scan new QR
- Check phone internet connection
- Disconnect and reconnect if status is stuck

## AI Not Replying

Check all three conditions:

1. Global auto reply is ON (`Settings`)
2. Contact AI is ON (`Contacts`)
3. A bot is available (assigned or default)

Also verify provider API key and model on the selected bot.

## No Contacts Showing

- Contacts appear only after real incoming WhatsApp messages.
- They are not pre-created manually.
