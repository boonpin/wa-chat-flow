# Contacts

Contacts are created automatically when WhatsApp messages arrive. Your team chooses
which configured AI agent should help each customer.

## Customer preferences

- **AI replies:** whether the customer’s future conversations may start with AI, subject
  to the workspace reply policy.
- **Default agent:** choose a sales, support, wholesale or other agent, or use the workspace
  default. This preference seeds future conversations and leaves active threads unchanged.

Take over or choose an agent for an active thread in [Inbox](./10-inbox.md). Its current
agent and lifecycle remain separate from the customer’s future preferences.

## Search, groups and bulk actions

Search by name or phone, and filter by AI preference. Select several contacts and use the
bulk bar to enable/disable AI for new conversations or assign an agent to that group.
Clear selection when done. These groups are selected by your team, not inferred by AI.

Maintain the available agents in [Settings → AI agents](./05-bots-management.md).
